NP Membership Benefits

Designed for non-profit organizations seeking to connect with the local business community.

  • Free membership with no annual fee.
  • Networking opportunities with other members.
  • Invitations to selected Chamber community events.
  • Access to general Chamber announcements and updates.

Bronze Membership Benefits

A starting point for businesses looking to establish valuable connections.

  • All applicable NP-level networking opportunities.
  • Business listing in the Chamber directory.
  • Member news and business updates.
  • Invitations to selected networking events.

Silver Membership Benefits

For businesses ready to strengthen their visibility and develop their capabilities.

  • All Bronze membership benefits.
  • Discounted access to selected training programmes.
  • Additional promotional opportunities.
  • Discounts on selected Chamber events.

Gold Membership Benefits

Our premium membership option for businesses seeking greater visibility and engagement.

  • All Silver membership benefits.
  • Priority business promotion opportunities.
  • Featured business spotlight opportunities.
  • Priority invitations to selected Chamber events.
  • Enhanced networking and partnership opportunities.
